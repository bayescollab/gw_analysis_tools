Convert speed of sound squared values to pressure and energy density values.

Takes in the pressure and energy density to start calculating for and a list of the baryon number density and cs^2 values to evaluate for. The output vectors are assumed to be empty prior to pass-in. Algorithm assumes that p_base, epsilon_base, and nb_list are all given in units of MeV. All inputs must start at the same baryon number density value.

Parameters
[in]p_basePressure at the start of bump (in MeV).
[in]epsilon_baseEnergy density at the start of bump (in MeV).
[in]nb_listList of baryon number density values (in MeV).
[in]cs2_bumpList of speed of sound squared values (in c).
[out]p_bumpVector to store new pressure values (in MeV).
[out]epsilon_bumpVector to store new energy density values (in MeV).

Convert EOS parameters to neutron star masses and tidal deformabilities.

Given the equation of state (EOS) parameters (bump width, offset, magnitude, and central baryon number densities), this function computes the corresponding astrophysical properties (masses and tidal deformabilities) for a neutron star binary.

Inject a bump into cs2 and get the corresponding pressures and energy densities.

Constructs cs^2 by stitching together the crust EOS, a single parabolic bump, and a plateau. Modifies pressure and energy density vectors according to cs^2 after bump injection.

Parameters
[out]pressure1Vector of pressures for star 1 (in MeV).
[out]pressure2Vector of pressures for star 2 (in MeV).
[out]epsilon1Vector of energy densities for star 1 (in MeV).
[out]epsilon2Vector of energy densities for star 2 (in MeV).
[in]paramsgen_params object containing EOS and bump parameters, including central baryon number densities.
